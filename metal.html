The mechanism

Studio’s cycle, wrapped in the enterprise layer.

The six-phase cycle is identical. What changes is everything around it, and it hardens at the interface layer on your own IT-approved AI stack, so no data leaves your control.

  • Pre-qualification. Validates genuine executive sponsorship before a contract is signed.
  • Terrain Map. Async intake of governance, regulatory constraints, and political landscape before ideation.
  • Red Team review. Every concept stress-tested against its likely internal opponents.
  • Governance mapping. Approval chain, veto players, and regulatory exposure, fully mapped.
  • Regulatory Runway. A realistic compliance timeline for each concept.
  • Capability gap analysis. Separates bridgeable gaps from existential blockers.

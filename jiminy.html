Jiminy · On the horizon

An always-on companion for the ideas you’d otherwise lose.

You notice something could be better, and the moment passes. Jiminy catches it: you name what you see, and it returns structured options, evidence, and tradeoffs. The machine explores. You judge.

A forward-looking vision. The model is being built now; the hardware is catching up.

The problem

The insight arrives, and then it’s gone.

The moment someone sees what’s broken is the start of every solution, and the easiest thing in the world to lose. There is no companion designed to catch that observation the instant it happens and hold it long enough to become something real.

The mechanism

Listen. Synthesize. Return options. Step back.

Jiminy listens when you notice something worth changing, runs the synthetic work, and brings back structured options, evidence, and pathways, then stops, because the choice is yours. Sovereign, context-aware, and built to run on-device.

Privacy is architecture

The pattern travels to the shared memory; the person never does. There is no mechanism to trace a pattern back to its source, a design constraint, not a policy.

Dignity is structural

You contribute to the collective intelligence. The collective never turns around and owns you, profiles you, or decides about you.

Judgment stays yours

Jiminy explores the options. What is true, worthy, and right remains, always, your call. It has your taste built in, it doesn’t replace it.

Why it’s plausible now

The device is already in a billion pockets.

Jiminy assumes continued advances in on-device AI, and the trajectory is moving toward it. The hardware that makes a private, always-on companion possible already exists; the model and the cycle behind it are what was missing.

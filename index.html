The problem

Most ideas don’t fail. They dissolve.

Not because the person wasn’t smart enough. Because there was no process to catch the moment, to shape it, pressure-test it, and hand it to someone who could build from it.

The observation happened. The moment passed. Nothing changed. Master is the infrastructure that catches that moment.

The best ideas inside every organization are dying in committee, not for lack of creativity, but because the system between idea and tested reality is broken.

What Master is

Insight in. Evidence out.

Six agents run every task that can be specified. One human owns every decision that can’t. The boundary is absolute.

Six agents do the work

Intake, Discovery, Concepts, Prototype, Validation, Handoff. Everything algorithmic (research, building, testing, documentation) runs without asking.

One human applies the taste

Your interface is seven judgments: continue, change, confirm, redirect, ship, kill, accept the risk. Nothing mechanical ever waits on you.

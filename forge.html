The problem

It’s not a weapons gap. It’s a time gap.

A major acquisition program takes 9 to 15 years from concept to fielding. The gap that decides modern conflicts is smaller and faster: the window where a forward unit identifies a need and has no structured way to turn it into a tested capability inside the mission.

Ukrainian drone teams field updated FPV designs in under two weeks. The equivalent US process runs 18 to 36 months. The resources aren’t missing. The structure at the unit level is.

The mechanism

Three tiers, each closer to the tip of the spear.

The core six-phase cycle runs identically in all three. What changes is the operating environment, the output format, and the cleared status of participants. The human judgment layer needs no connectivity to function. AI provides compression, not the method.

Delivers a Field Card in hours, not days.
1–2

Observe, frame & discover

A trained operator turns a specific, bounded field observation into a one-page problem frame, then pulls prior attempts and analogous situations, under an hour with AI, structured and manual without it.

3–4

Concept & prototype

Two to four alternatives, each testable at squad level within 24 hours. A procedural change is rehearsed; an equipment adaptation is built from what’s on hand. The prototype must be runnable, not theoretical.

5–6

Validate & passport

The alternatives run against the actual situation. Observed results, simple scoring, then a two-page Battle Passport ready to move up the chain or across units without losing fidelity.

The proof

The pilot result won’t be close.

The cleanest evidence is an asymmetric pilot: a FORGE-trained unit against an untrained control, measured on one number: how many testable concepts each generated, tested, and documented in 90 days. The precedent is already on the battlefield.

Velocity wins

Ukraine’s drone operators and Israel’s Unit 81 out-iterate centralized procurement not through resources but through structure: small teams, clear decision authority, testing over analysis.

Works without the network

The six-phase sequence is a thinking structure. A FORGE-trained operator runs it on a laminated card set, offline, under time pressure measured in hours.

The operator decides

AI does the synthetic heavy lifting, then stops. The warfighter knows the ground, the moment, and which path is walkable. The decision is never the machine’s.
